# Vendor Risk Management (VRM) Policy

## 1. Purpose
Ensure third-party tools and services used in the environment are reviewed for security risk.

## 2. Scope
Applies to vendors/tools such as:
- Security tooling
- Agents/forwarders
- Cloud or SaaS services used for lab work

## 3. Policy Requirements
- Vendor/tool must be assessed before adoption:
  - Purpose and data access
  - Authentication method
  - Logging capability
  - Known vulnerabilities/reputation
- Risk rating must be recorded.
- Ongoing monitoring required for critical vendors/tools.

## 4. Evidence
- Vendor assessment checklist
- Risk register entry

## 5. Review Frequency
Annually, or upon major changes.
